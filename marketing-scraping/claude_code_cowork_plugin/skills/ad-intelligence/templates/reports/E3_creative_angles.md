# Creative Angle Audit ({{CLIENT}})

> **Inputs:** your Meta Ad Library, Google Search ads, Google Ads Transparency, and LinkedIn Ad Library raw runs. The report step reads them and builds the taxonomy. **Ad duration (Google Ads Transparency `firstShownAt` to `lastShownAt`) is the "what works" proxy:** the longest-running ads are the ones that stayed profitable enough to keep paying for.
>
> **What a strong report contains:** a lettered angle taxonomy (A to H) with example copy per angle and a frequency; a duration-weighted effectiveness ranking (which angles the longest-running ads use); a platform comparison (Meta vs Google vs LinkedIn: who advertises where, which angles per platform, the platform gap); a prioritised gap analysis (underused angles that map to high customer relevance); a headline bank plus anti-patterns; and a creative brief (positioning, the first angles to test, seasonal timing). Close with actionable takeaways.

## 4. Gap analysis: underused angles with high customer relevance
_Prioritised: the angles nobody runs that map to real customer pains, trust signals, and fairness concerns. If you have run the pain-and-messaging tool, pull its customer pains and trust signals in here; otherwise infer relevance from the ad copy itself. Rank by priority._

## 5. Headline bank + anti-patterns
_Reusable hooks (pain-led, geo, fairness). Anti-patterns to avoid: the arms races, the generic claims, the over-claims the client can't back._

## 6. Creative brief
_Positioning. The 3 angles to test first. Seasonal timing (from the search-intelligence tool's 12-month volume trend, when available). Budget split across segments and seasons. Actionable takeaways._
